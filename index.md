## Projects

### [DataLens — Autonomous Multi-Agent BI Platform](https://github.com/jrbickelh/enterprise-datalens)

Open-source autonomous business intelligence platform that replaces traditional dashboard workflows with a natural language interface backed by a multi-agent AI system. Built on LangGraph with hybrid RAG (BM25 + ChromaDB vector search), YAML semantic layer, NetworkX knowledge graph, and self-healing SQL against DuckDB. 120+ tests, sub-3s query latency, ~$0.02–0.08 per query.

**Stack:** Python, LangGraph, LangChain, DuckDB, ChromaDB, Azure OpenAI, Streamlit, scikit-learn, Plotly, Langfuse

### [ORCA — Orchestrated Reasoning for Capital Allocation](https://github.com/jrbickelh/orca)

LLM-enhanced algorithmic trading pipeline for crypto and equity markets. Decomposes trading decisions into specialized agents — technical analysis, multi-source sentiment aggregation, risk gating with Kelly criterion and circuit breakers, portfolio sizing, and paper execution. Bayesian hyperparameter optimization with Optuna.

**Stack:** Python, LangGraph, FastAPI, Alpaca, MCP, Optuna

### [Nertia — Personal AI Agent](https://github.com/jrbickelh/Nertia)

Personal assistant featuring multi-tool orchestration, RAG-based persistent memory, and proactive scheduling capabilities.

**Stack:** Python, Claude Agent SDK, ChromaDB

### [BlueBERT — Sentiment-Driven Market Prediction](https://github.com/jrbickelh/BlueBERT)

Capstone research project investigating the correlation between social media sentiment and stock market performance. Leverages BERT for sentiment analysis on financial text, integrated with time-series forecasting to generate trading signals.
